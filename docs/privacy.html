Privacy Policy

Buster, operated by Daniel Engel. Last updated October 3, 2026.

What we collect

  • The mobile numbers and first names of the two enrolled participants, and whether each replied YES or opted out.
  • The content of texts sent to the Buster number: amounts, optional short notes, and commands.
  • Message timestamps and the message IDs our SMS provider assigns.

How we use it

Only to run the tally: record entries, compute the balance, and send the replies and alerts described in the SMS Terms. We don't use it for advertising, profiling, or anything else.

Text messages

You opt in by texting START (or YES) to Buster at (970) 707-0992, as described in the SMS Terms. Message frequency varies with use, typically a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, or STOP at any time to opt out.

Where it's stored

Entries are stored in a database hosted by Cloudflare. Text messages are sent and received through Twilio, which processes message content and phone numbers to deliver them. These providers act only as service providers for Buster.

Sharing

Mobile numbers are not shared with third parties. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with or sold to any third party, affiliate, or lead generator. Information is disclosed to the service providers above only as needed to deliver messages, or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Entries are kept so the tally and its history stay accurate. To have your data deleted, email the address below.

Opting out

Reply STOP to the Buster number at any time to stop all messages.
