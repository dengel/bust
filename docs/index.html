Buster

SMS Terms and Opt-In. Last updated October 3, 2026.

What it is

Buster is a private, non-commercial text message tally for two people who share expenses. A participant texts the Buster number with an amount they paid (for example, 42.50 dinner), and Buster replies with the running balance of who owes whom. When the balance reaches $100, both participants get a text saying it's time to settle up.

Buster is run by Daniel Engel as a sole proprietor for personal use. It has exactly two participants, both known personally to the operator. It does not send marketing or promotional messages, and it does not accept sign-ups from the public.

How you opt in

Only two people can use Buster. The operator enters their two mobile numbers in the Buster application, with each person's permission. Texts from any other number are ignored and get no reply. Numbers are never purchased, shared, or collected from any other source.

Enrollment is a two-step confirmation by text:

  1. The participant sends their first text to the Buster number. Buster replies with this welcome message and records nothing until they answer:
    Buster: Reply YES to get shared expense tally texts with [other participant]. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
  2. The participant replies YES to consent, and gets this confirmation:
    Buster: You're enrolled. Text an amount you paid, e.g. 42.50 dinner. Msg freq varies. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
    Or they reply NO to decline, and get:
    Buster: OK, you won't get Buster texts. Reply YES anytime to join.

Until a participant replies YES, Buster logs nothing for them and sends them no alerts. By replying YES, you agree to receive:

  • Replies to texts you send (confirmations, balances, history).
  • Alerts triggered by the other participant: a BUST alert when the balance reaches the limit, and a settled notice when someone records that the balance was paid.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with use, typically a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You'll get this confirmation and no further texts:

Buster: You're unsubscribed and will get no more messages. Reply START to resubscribe.

Reply HELP for help:

Buster: Shared expense tally. Email multisite@gmail.com for help. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
