What it is

Buster is a private, non-commercial text message tally for two people who share expenses. A participant texts the Buster number with an amount they paid (for example, 42.50 dinner), and Buster replies with the running balance of who owes whom. When the balance reaches $100, both participants get a text saying it's time to settle up.

Buster is run by Daniel Engel as a sole proprietor for personal use. It has exactly two participants, both known personally to the operator. It does not send marketing or promotional messages, and it does not accept sign-ups from the public.

How you opt in

You opt in by texting the Buster number yourself. Buster never texts anyone first.

  1. Text START (or YES) to (970) 707-0992. You'll get this confirmation:
    Buster: You're opted in. Text an amount you paid, e.g. 42.50 dinner. Msg freq varies. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
  2. If your first text to the number is anything else, Buster replies with this prompt and records nothing until you answer:
    Buster: Reply YES to get shared expense tally texts with [other participant]. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
    Reply YES to opt in, or NO to decline:
    Buster: OK, you won't get Buster texts. Reply YES anytime to join.

Buster is limited to two participants, who share the number with each other directly. As a security measure, texts from numbers other than those two are not processed. Numbers are never purchased, shared, or collected from any other source.

Until a participant replies YES, Buster logs nothing for them and sends them no alerts. By replying YES, you agree to receive:

  • Replies to texts you send (confirmations, balances, history).
  • Alerts triggered by the other participant: a BUST alert when the balance reaches the limit, and a settled notice when someone records that the balance was paid.
