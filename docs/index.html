bust

SMS Terms and Opt-In. Last updated October 3, 2026.

What it is

bust is a private, non-commercial text message tally for two people who share expenses. A participant texts the bust number with an amount they paid (for example, 42.50 dinner), and bust replies with the running balance of who owes whom. When the balance reaches $100, both participants get a text saying it's time to settle up.

bust is run by Daniel Engel as a sole proprietor for personal use. It has exactly two participants, both known personally to the operator. It does not send marketing or promotional messages, and it does not accept sign-ups from the public.

How you opt in

Each participant gives their mobile number directly to the operator and agrees to receive bust messages. Their number is then added as one of the two enrolled participants. Enrollment is confirmed when the participant sends their first text to the bust number. Numbers are never purchased, shared, or collected from any other source. Texts from any number that is not enrolled are ignored and get no reply.

By opting in, you agree to receive:

  • Replies to texts you send (confirmations, balances, history).
  • Alerts triggered by the other participant: a BUST alert when the balance reaches the limit, and a settled notice when someone records that the balance was paid.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with use, typically a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You'll get one confirmation and no further texts. Reply START to resume. Reply HELP for help, or email multisite@gmail.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Settled: Sam paid Alex $104.25. Tally reset to $0.
Text an amount you paid, e.g. "42.50 dinner". Commands: BALANCE, UNDO, HISTORY, PAID.
